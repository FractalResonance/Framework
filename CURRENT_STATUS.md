- It is not the publication source for new manuscripts.
- It is not a license to cite superseded stadium, collapse, Born-rule, or
  Lambda claims without checking their current living carrier.

The current program retains historical work rather than deleting it, while
marking which material remains active, frontier, revision-pending, or archived.
